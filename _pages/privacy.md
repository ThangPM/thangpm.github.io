This is a personal research and engineering website hosted on GitHub Pages.

## Your choice

Google Analytics is loaded **only after you allow analytics**. Your choice is stored in your browser's local storage. Declining does not prevent you from reading any part of this website. Advertising signals and advertising personalization are disabled.

<button type="button" class="profile-link-primary" data-analytics-settings hidden>Change analytics preference</button>

You can also clear this site's browser storage to be asked again. Opting out stops further Google Analytics measurement on this site; it does not delete previously collected data or existing cookies. Content blockers may prevent analytics even when you opt in.

## What analytics can show

With permission, Google Analytics processes usage information such as pages viewed, referrals, device/browser information, and approximate country, region, or city. Location estimates may be inaccurate, especially with VPNs. Reports are aggregated and may have reporting thresholds.

**I do not receive visitors' names, email addresses, employers, or professions from analytics.** This site does not use visitor-identification or fingerprinting services. Google Analytics may use cookies and pseudonymous identifiers; it is not fully anonymous. See [Google's privacy information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) for details.

GitHub and external services linked or embedded on this site may process technical request information under their own policies. See the [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

For privacy questions, [contact me by email](mailto:{{ site.email }}).
